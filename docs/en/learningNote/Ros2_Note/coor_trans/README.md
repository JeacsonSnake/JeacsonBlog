## Brief

These are my notes on the ROS2 tool **coordinate transform (tf)**. They cover:

- the concept of coordinate transforms and where you use them
- the definition format of the two important message carriers for coordinate transforms, `TransformStamped` & `PointStamped` (based on *topic communication*)
- the two sides of a coordinate transform (broadcaster and listener)
- the coordinate transform tools (a closer look at the related commands, plus debugging tools and so on)

For the software, hardware and technical background, see [A brief introduction to the ROS2 learning-notes collection](../README.md)
