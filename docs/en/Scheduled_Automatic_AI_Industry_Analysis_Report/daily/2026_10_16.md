## 📄 arXiv Papers — cs.AI / cs.LG / cs.CL

1. **[Co-Evolving Acting and Forecasting for Language Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20597)** | cs.AI | Haofei Yu, Yixing Chen
   *Training language agents is limited by the cost of long-horizon rollouts, while standard agentic RL learns only from a terminal scalar reward and wastes the supervision available at intermediate steps; this work lets "acting" and "forecasting" co-evolve to put that intermediate supervision to use.*
2. **[One Task, Many Interfaces: Simple Cross-Interface Consistency Training Improves LLM Agent Robustness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20576)** | cs.LG | Artin Tajdini, Manpreet Kaur
   *Post-training ties an LLM agent's abilities to the exact interface it was trained on — rename an action or a token and performance drops; the authors propose simple cross-interface consistency training that markedly improves robustness without changing task semantics.*
3. **[Beyond LLM Judge: Mechanical Verification for Agentic Autoformalization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20573)** | cs.LO | Anna Mei, Michael R. Douglas
   *For Lean formalization, this proposes combining LLM semantic review with mechanical verification, replacing "let the LLM be the judge" as the sole verification approach.*
4. **[Player-1: Learning to Play from Synthetic Experience](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20584)** | cs.CV | Mingyu Ouyang, Yiran Wang
   *Computer-use agents can already read the screen and drive keyboard and mouse, but games with rich visuals and varied mechanics remain hard; this work trains them to act in game environments from synthetic experience.*
5. **[Manacá-1B-Instruct-2: Reproducible Safety Alignment for a Native Brazilian-Portuguese LLM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20570)** | cs.CL | Bruno Leonardo Santos Menezes, Carlos Leonardo Souza Cardoso
   *A reproducible study of safety alignment for an open Brazilian-Portuguese small model: it publishes negative DPO results, a controlled combination sweep, and one silent failure mode of LLM judges.*
