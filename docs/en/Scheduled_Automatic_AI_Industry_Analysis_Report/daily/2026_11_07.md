## 📄 arXiv Papers — cs.AI / cs.LG / cs.CL
*(The 5 papers most relevant to agents / reasoning / LLM training and inference; latest submission date in this batch: 11/05)*

- **[SwarmEvolve: Latent Agent Swarms for Evolutionary Discovery and Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08483)** | cs.AI | Qile Jiang, George Em Karniadakis
  *LLM-guided evolutionary search improves programs, but the long chain-of-thought generated each round is slow and expensive. The authors put multiple agents into latent space to co-evolve, cutting redundant reasoning overhead.*
- **[Scientist World: Grow a Research World for AI-Scientists](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08478)** | cs.AI | Taiming Lu, Sachin Konan
  *Existing literature is "readable but not runnable" for AI-Scientists, so the paper builds a research world that actually runs, giving AI scientists an interactive environment of problems and experiments.*
- **[ReproBridge: Building Reproducibility into ML Research Artifacts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08452)** | cs.AI | Yubo Wang, Rui Meng
  *Reproduction success often hinges on whether a coding agent can fill in the details missing between paper and code. The authors build reproducibility directly into the structure of research artifacts.*
- **[Benchmarks as Software: Terminal-Bench and the Case for Continuous Benchmarks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08417)** | cs.AI | E. Kelly Buchanan, Crystal Zhou
  *Coding agents are the most economically valuable landing spot for LLMs, but benchmarks go stale as models get stronger — some correct answers are marked wrong by old verifiers. The paper argues for maintaining benchmarks as software.*
- **[Spectral and Subspace Dynamics of LoRA in Supervised Fine-Tuning and Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08414)** | cs.LG | Chenruo Liu, Zihan Wang
  *Under the same optimizer settings, LoRA's low-rank incremental dynamics are not the same across the SFT and RL stages; the paper uses singular-value tracking to explain the spectral and subspace differences.*
