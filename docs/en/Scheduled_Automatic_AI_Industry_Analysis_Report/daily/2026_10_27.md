  *Over 70% of LLM RL post-training cost goes into autoregressive rollout generation. The authors speed that part up with rollout-structure-aware n-gram speculative decoding, without sacrificing generation quality.*
- **[J-Comm: Jacobian-Guided Latent Communication between LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32871)** | cs.CL | Xiangwei Wang, Wei Wang, Sachith Seneviratne
  *Instead of passing natural language between multiple agents, they use Jacobian-guided latent-space communication, handing intermediate reasoning directly to an external model for reuse.*
- **[Where Rounding Happens Matters: Rounding-Location Alignment Stabilizes LLM Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32951)** | cs.LG | Yijing Ke, Tianle Cai, Ruosong Wang
  *In systems where training and inference are separated, mismatched rounding locations shift the RL objective even when the weights are identical; aligning the rounding locations stabilizes training markedly.*
- **[Interpretable sparse fine-tuning with TopKLoRA enables faithful circuit discovery](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32866)** | cs.LG | Marek Masiak, Andrzej Szablewski, Nicola Cancedda
  *TopKLoRA does sparse fine-tuning, and can locate and strip backdoor circuits out of a model trained on poisoned data while staying interpretable.*
