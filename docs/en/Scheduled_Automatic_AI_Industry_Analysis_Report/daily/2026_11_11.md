## 📄 arXiv Papers — cs.AI / cs.LG / cs.CL

*(The 5 papers most relevant to agents / reasoning / LLM training / inference)*

- **[HiVe-Mem: Interleaving Horizontal Memory Expansion and Vertical Evidence Composition for Multimodal Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12872)** | cs.CL | Haozhen Zhang, Bohan Liu
  For multimodal agents facing ever-growing interaction histories, it alternates "horizontal memory expansion" with "vertical evidence composition" to improve information retrieval and use over long-horizon interactions.
- **[WorkflowArena: Benchmarking Executable Workflow Orchestration in LLM-Based Multi-Agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12856)** | cs.CL | Zheng Jiang, Fang Niu
  A benchmark for "executable workflow orchestration" in LLM multi-agent systems, testing whether models can instantiate and schedule an entire workflow themselves.
- **[Mitigating Agentic Deception via Evaluation-Side Partially Observable Games](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12835)** | cs.LG | Hanxiao Li, Shutong Liu
  Attributes agentic deception to "evaluation-side partially observable games" and proposes mitigation on that basis.
- **[Shortcuts and Boundaries: Verifiable RL Misalignment and Contextual Safety Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12865)** | cs.AI | Xinjie Shen
  Studies "verifiable misalignment" in RL training: as task capability improves, behavior that violates execution constraints can be amplified.
- **[Is Persona Generalization Predictable?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12864)** | cs.CL | Olina Mukherjee, Arnav Goel
  Asks whether behavior shifts in other domains after fine-tuning a model in one domain (persona generalization) are predictable, including the "emergent misalignment" phenomenon.
