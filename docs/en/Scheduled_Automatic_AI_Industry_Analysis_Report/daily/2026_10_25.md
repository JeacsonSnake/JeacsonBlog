## 📄 arXiv Papers — cs.AI / cs.LG / cs.CL
*(5 papers most relevant to agents / reasoning / LLM training / inference)*

- **[EpiSEM-Bench: Evaluating Safety and Evidence Recovery in LLM Agents under Missing Evidence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31650)** | cs.AI | Qijin Zhang, Hanzhen Wang
  *Tool-using agents rely on environmental facts to judge whether an action is permitted; when a tool response **omits** a key safety fact, the agent may go ahead with a forbidden operation. This bench specifically tests safety and evidence recovery in such "missing evidence" scenarios.*
- **[Same Luck, Different Choice: Twin-World Credit Assignment for Agentic Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31544)** | cs.LG | Zeropy Surio, Tobias Engel
  *Agentic RL rewards only the whole episode, yet the policy needs credit assigned to every intermediate decision. The authors use counterfactual branches to build a "twin-world" comparison for estimating per-step credit.*
- **[Questioning Instrumental Convergence in RLVR: Checkpoint Evidence from OLMo-3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31624)** | cs.LG | Barış Alparslan, Sandy Tanwisuth
  *Uses multiple checkpoints of the open model OLMo-3 to test the "instrumental convergence" hypothesis: as capability grows, do subgoals such as self-preservation, power-seeking, deception and resistance to oversight really emerge spontaneously?*
- **[Direct Pass@$k$ Optimization for Thought Pruning: Balancing Quality and Diversity in Test-Time Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31667)** | cs.AI | Mahdi Salmani, Adel Javanmard
  *Test-time scaling generates a large pool of candidate solutions and then selects among them, which is expensive. This paper directly optimizes pass@k to prune chains of thought, balancing quality against diversity.*
- **[Low-Bit N-Gram Embeddings for Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31646)** | cs.CL | Stephen Chung, Wenyu Du
  *Many recent open-weight models use n-gram embedding tables to improve results, but those tables are huge. This paper pushes n-gram embeddings to low bit-width, directly cutting the memory footprint of open-weight models.*
