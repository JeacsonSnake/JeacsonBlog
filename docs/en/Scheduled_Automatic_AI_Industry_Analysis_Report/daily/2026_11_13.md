## 📄 arXiv Papers — cs.AI / cs.LG / cs.CL

- **[AutoScaffold: AI-Guided Scaffolding for Reinforcement Learning of Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14515)** | cs.LG | Zijian Zhang, Rizhen Hu
  *RLVR uses only outcome rewards, compressing each attempt into a single score and wasting most of the process information. This paper uses AI to automatically generate scaffolding structures that organize the training process, so process signals are actually put to use.*
- **[ReTrace: Distilling Tool-Using Agents into Planner-Free Workflows](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14492)** | cs.AI | Kaiyuan Hou, Yang Ping Kuo
  *Enterprises run the same SOPs over and over, and the planner has to re-reason every time — costly and unstable. The authors distill a tool-calling agent into a fixed planner-free workflow, keeping tool capabilities while removing per-run planning.*
- **[A Theory of Policy Optimization in Post-Training: Coverage Collapse and Reference Resets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14467)** | cs.LG | Audrey Huang, Nan Jiang
  *A theory for policy optimization in post-training: it identifies "coverage collapse" during training and proposes reference reset as a recovery mechanism.*
- **[Comparative Analysis of Agent Evaluation Frameworks: Stability, Detection, and Discovery in Enterprise Analytics Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14493)** | cs.AI | Howard Zhang, Chuck Arvin
  *Enterprise analytics agents must reliably detect failures inside complex multi-tool workflows; the authors compare several evaluation approaches with a three-stage framework.*
- **[Continual-ARC: Measuring Skill Acquisition over a Working Lifetime](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14505)** | cs.LG | Daniel Lenton
  *Existing continual-learning benchmarks only look at single tasks and cannot measure skill accumulation "over a working lifetime"; this paper stretches the evaluation horizon to long-term skill acquisition.*
