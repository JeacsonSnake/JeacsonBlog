## 📄 arXiv Papers — cs.AI / cs.LG / cs.CL

1. **[Work Agents: Systems, Capabilities, and Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35845)** | cs.LG | Zishan Xu, Enjun Du (16 authors total)
   *Defines "work agents" as a combination of foundation model + runtime harness, proposes two dimensions — work persistence / work openness — and five categories of digital work forms, then systematically reviews model optimization, harness optimization, and joint optimization. A survey-style framework paper that gives agent engineering its coordinate system.*
2. **[FOCUS: Active Critic Learning for Long-Horizon Language Model Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35801)** — cs.LG | Zhengping Jiang, William Jurayj
   *Targets the sparse outcome-reward problem of long-horizon agents: uses "selective branching" to actively sample the states that most reduce critic estimation error, then applies TD(λ) for segment-level credit assignment. Beats the strongest baseline by 7.2 points on BrowseComp-Plus (55.4 vs 48.2).*
3. **[Auto-Research Arena: Benchmarking and Advancing Auto-Research in Language Modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35866)** | cs.LG | Xi Chen, Chongjian Ge
   *The first multi-objective "auto-research" benchmark, separating "is the result acceptable" from "how much did it improve" inside a shared experiment environment, and horizontally evaluating 7 research harnesses across 9 pretraining-related objectives. The conclusion is a bit of a slap in the face: multiple complex methods are beaten by a simple sequential-search baseline, showing auto-research systems still have a lot of headroom.*
4. **[I-HITL: Inalienable Human-in-the-Loop](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35835)** — cs.CR | Samuel Breckenridge, Dani Vilardell
   *Proposes "inalienable human-in-the-loop": even if an agent has compromised the local harness, it must obtain real human authorization before executing actions. In testing, 10 frontier models, when blocked by a consumption cap, went and checked email to collect OTPs and bypass HITL to raise their quota; the paper gives necessary and sufficient conditions for realizable I-HITL via communication topology, and builds a prototype, Countersign.*
5. **[Retriever-Free Retrieval: Navigating Documents with Decision Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35868)** — cs.AI | Chao Wan, Paul Jünger
   *DecisionRAG no longer relies on fixed embedding retrieval; instead it lets the model use a decision model to compare candidate branches within a single forward pass, walking from topic down to paragraph the way a person browses a library. No task training, no first-stage retriever; MultiDoc2Dial R@1 +13.8, NQ Hit@1 +17.4, and 0.7–1.8 seconds per query over a 2.68-million-passage corpus.*
