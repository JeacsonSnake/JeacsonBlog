  *Has visual agents repeatedly play-test a game and record their progress, decisions and reflections, then iterates on the game accordingly so auto-generated games are more replayable and give a stronger sense of mastery.*

  *An action-level analysis of 12 robot manipulation benchmarks: the acting agent declares a local intent at each step, and the critic judges outcomes and failure modes against that intent, closing the blind spot of episode-level success rates.*

  *Uses natural-language "skills" to suppress bad habits in teacher trajectories and amplify effective behaviors, with an LLM agent autonomously designing interventions and selecting data for end-to-end autonomous post-training.*
