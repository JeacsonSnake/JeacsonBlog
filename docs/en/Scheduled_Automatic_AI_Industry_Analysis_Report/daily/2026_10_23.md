## 📄 arXiv papers — cs.AI / cs.LG / cs.CL

- **[LongZero: Enhancing Long-Context Reasoning in LLMs from Zero Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30132)** | cs.CL | Zhennan Wan, Jianfei Chen
  Reinforcing long-context reasoning with RL usually needs long, diverse and verifiable training data, and human annotation is extremely expensive. The authors propose building this kind of training signal from zero data, bypassing the annotation bottleneck.
- **[Environment Duels: A Microcosm of LLM Co-Evolution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30116)** | cs.CL | Shibo Jin, Zhiqiu Xu
  Frontier models evolve by learning from each other's output. This paper puts inter-model "co-evolution" into a controllable micro-arena and looks at how each model performs both as a creator and as a competitor.
- **[Quant-Arena: Benchmarking LLM Agents for Quantitative Trading Research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30113)** | cs.CL | Houde Qian, Zheng Jiang
  Quantitative research is essentially a loop: propose a hypothesis → turn it into an executable signal → backtest → decide whether to keep it. Existing benchmarks only score formulas or single decisions; Quant-Arena instead scores the entire agent research pipeline.
- **[Did the Problem Actually Get Harder? Testing LLMs on Paired P and NP-Complete Problems Hardened by Adversarial Multi-Agent Rewriting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30103)** | cs.CL | Nurul Labib Sayeedi, Md. Faiyaz Abdullah Sayeedi
  Uses adversarial multi-agent rewriting to "harden" paired P / NPC problems, testing whether models reason or just memorize answers — genuine reasoning ability should be able to spot that boundary which differs by a single structural detail.
- **[Multimodal Embodied Agents: A Unified Capability-centric Perspective from Computer-Use to Robot-Use](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30108)** | cs.RO | Yanzhe Chen, Ziyi Yang
  A survey: it unifies computer-use agents and robotic systems under a "multimodal embodied agent" framework, organizing capability dimensions around four elements — reasoning, memory, tools and feedback.
