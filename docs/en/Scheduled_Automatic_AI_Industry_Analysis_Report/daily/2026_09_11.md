## 📄 arXiv Papers — cs.AI / cs.LG / cs.CL
*(5 papers most relevant to agents / reasoning / LLM training & inference)*

- **[JarvisGUI: Towards Cross-Device GUI Agents with Dynamic Task Composition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10451)** | cs.AI | Zixiang Chen, Yuheng Lu
  Pointing out that existing GUI-agent benchmarks almost only evaluate single-device scenarios, it proposes a cross-device, cross-platform-workflow GUI agent and a dynamic task-composition method.
- **[ConvMem: Convolutional Memory for Long-Context Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10441)** | cs.AI | Hongming Zhang, Zhaozhen Gu
  Replaces fixed-length state updated by segment-by-segment iteration with convolutional memory, easing LLMs' capacity bottleneck under ultra-long context.
- **[Forgetting Only What Matters: Layer-Selective Unlearning toward Robust LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10439)** | cs.LG | Ravi Ranjan, Olivera Kotevska
  Points out that most machine-unlearning methods treat parameters with a "broad sweep", and proposes a layer-selective LLM-unlearning scheme.
- **[Building Multilingual Bridges: Data Mixing as the Pillar of Generalization for In-Language Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10445)** | cs.CL | Mehrnaz Mofakhami, Ananya Sahu
  Current reasoning models tend to reason in English regardless of the question's language; this paper studies how data-mixing strategy affects generalization to non-English native-language reasoning.
- **[IdeaAMBIG: Benchmarking Implementation-Critical Gaps in Research-Idea Specifications](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10539)** | cs.CL | Yiling Ma, Yilun Zhao
  Evaluates whether a "research-idea specification" is sufficient to be faithfully implemented—novel and reasonable, yet the method description may still not be enough to reproduce.
