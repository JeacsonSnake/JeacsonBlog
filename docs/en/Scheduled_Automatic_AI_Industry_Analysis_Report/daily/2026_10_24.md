## 📄 arXiv papers — cs.AI / cs.LG / cs.CL

- **[Direct Pass@$k$ Optimization for Thought Pruning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31667)** | cs.AI | Mahdi Salmani, Adel Javanmard
  *Test-time scaling generates a large number of candidate solutions, and running all of them is too expensive. This work directly optimizes Pass@k to prune the chain of thought, balancing quality against diversity.*
- **[EpiSEM-Bench: Safety and Evidence Recovery in LLM Agents under Missing Evidence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31650)** | cs.AI | Qijin Zhang, Hanzhen Wang
  *When a tool returns without a critical safety fact, a tool-using agent may still perform actions that should be forbidden. This benchmark specifically measures such "overreach under missing evidence" and evaluates recovery ability.*
- **[Causal Chain of Thought: Mitigating Attention Leakage via Context Isolation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31639)** | cs.LG | Gabriele Dominici, Teague McMillan
  *CoT reasoning steps do not actually drive the answer causally (dense attention causes "attention leakage"); context isolation lets each reasoning step see only what it should see.*
- **[Low-Bit N-Gram Embeddings for Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31646)** | cs.CL | Stephen Chung, Wenyu Du
  *Low-bit quantization of the n-gram embedding tables that open-weight models commonly use, to cut memory footprint.*
- **[Can an Agent Debug a Web Corpus? Agentic Auditing of Slovak FineWeb2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31636)** | cs.CL | Marek Šuppa, Ivan Vykopal
  *Treating the LLM as a "corpus debugger" rather than a classifier: a tool-using agent actively probes the corpus to find the failure modes of a multilingual pipeline on a single language.*
