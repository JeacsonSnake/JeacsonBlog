## 📄 arXiv Papers — cs.AI / cs.LG / cs.CL
*(the 5 most relevant to agent / reasoning / LLM training / inference)*

- **[From extreme weather to agricultural consequences with agentic AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40641)** | physics.ao-ph | Minghan Yu, Mengyu Dai
  Weather forecasts can say a few days ahead where a storm will hit, but not which crops will be damaged or how large the losses will be. This paper uses agentic AI to link extreme weather events to agricultural consequences, closing the last hop from forecast to impact.
- **[Collaborative AI agents for autonomous experimental research with solid-state quantum spins](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40589)** | cs.AI | El Mustapha Mansouri, Eikichi Kimura
  Picture an autonomous lab that can carry a whole study through on its own: pose the question, run the experiments, write the paper. This paper shows collaborative AI agents doing autonomous experimental research on solid-state quantum spin systems.
- **[ShallowDefend: Harnessing Shallow Safety Alignment for Multi-Turn Defender](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40627)** | cs.CL | Qiyao Ma, Xiaodong Liu
  Safety alignment makes a model refuse a harmful request outright, yet split the same request across several turns and the success rate is still far too high. This paper uses the model's "shallow" safety-alignment signals to build a multi-turn defense.
- **[TextSeal2: Distortion-Free LLM Watermarking with Copula Mixing and Memoryless Races](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40613)** | cs.CR | Tom Sander, Pierre Fernandez
  Distortion-free watermarking for large models; the crux is keeping the signal alive under speculative decoding: tokens replaced by rejected drafts keep their watermark marks using the same batch of keys.
- **[Rethinking Neural Thickets Without Weight Perturbations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40649)** | cs.LG | Nicolas Dufour, Elouan Gardès
  Reproduces the "neural thickets" hypothesis (pretrained language models sit in dense neighborhoods of task experts that random Gaussian perturbations can dig out), and re-examines the claim without applying any weight perturbations.
