- **[Tev: Scaling Long-Horizon Agentic Environments with Dense Progress Rewards](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18868)** | cs.CL | Wenbo Hu, Shraman Pramanick
  Long-horizon agents need to work autonomously for hours to days, and sparse training signal is the bottleneck; this work scales long-horizon agentic environments with dense progress rewards.
- **[Hybrid Reasoning Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18861)** | cs.CL | Jiaru Zou, Yunzhe Qi
  Explores how to build agents that "think beyond human language while still acting in external environments through language", a hybrid paradigm between implicit reasoning and explicit linguistic action.
- **[Two Clocks: Why Larger Rollout Batches Learn Slower but Fail on Schedule in RLVR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18853)** | cs.LG | Yuante Li, Junwei Quan
  Offers a mechanistic explanation for the phenomenon in reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) where increasing the rollout batch learns more slowly yet still collapses on the given schedule.
- **[ChunkAct: Action Chunking for Agentic Robotics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18874)** | cs.RO | Bingxin Xu, Wenrui Bao
  Putting LLM agents into robot control loops requires combining perception, locomotion and other skills; action chunking improves the executability of agentic robotics.
- **[DoubtLess: Training-Free Contrastive Decoding for Efficient Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18862)** | cs.CL | Minghao Luo, Liang Chen
  Reasoning models score high through long CoT but often spend extra tokens; this method is training-free and uses contrastive decoding to cut redundant reasoning chains.
