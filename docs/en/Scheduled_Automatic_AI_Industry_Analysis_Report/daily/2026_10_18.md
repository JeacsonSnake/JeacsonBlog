  *Has a builder agent pre-organize raw documents into a hierarchical filesystem workspace for later queries, optimizing downstream answer accuracy directly with RL; beats the strongest baseline by 33 points on BrowseComp-Plus and by +31 points zero-shot on BRIGHT.*
- **[Mosaic: Multi-Session Agentic Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22174)** | cs.CL | Yilong Xu, Minrui Xu, Xinyu Zhou
  *Observes that context compression and subagent forking make real trajectories span multiple sessions, while existing agentic RL assumes a single session; proposes critic-free hierarchical advantage estimation — on BrowseComp it is both more session-efficient and more accurate than broadcast-style advantages.*
- **[Learning to Steer LLM Agents and Multi-Agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22316)** | cs.LG | Leyang Shen, Yaxin Gong, Yang Zhang
  *Targets "goal drift" in long-horizon execution by training an online supervisor agent to decide when to use a low-cost fast path and when to intervene with a high-cost slow one; joint pairwise-ranking + GRPO training transfers across tasks and executors.*
- **[KnowGPO: Knowledge-Grouped Policy Optimization for Search Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22331)** | cs.AI | Siqi Chen, Zhengxi Lu, Yuchen Yan
  *Groups trajectories by "knowledge already acquired" rather than surface observations to assign process rewards, solving credit assignment in deep search; an 8B model scores 3.8–4.2 points above GRPO, GiGPO and IGPO on Xbench / BrowseComp-ZH / GAIA.*
