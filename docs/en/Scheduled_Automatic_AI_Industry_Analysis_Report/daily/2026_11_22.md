## 📄 arXiv Papers — cs.AI / cs.LG / cs.CL
*(The 5 papers most relevant to agents / reasoning / LLM training / inference; latest batch submitted 11/19)*

- **[PlayCraft: Designing Engaging Games from Agent Play Experience](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26624)** | cs.AI | Weihao Cheng, et al.
  *Has visual agents actually play-test AI-generated games, then iterates the design based on the sense of mastery, tackling generated games that are playable but not engaging.*
- **[AgentRoboCritic: Understanding the Capability of Frontier Agents on Direct Robot Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26613)** | cs.RO | Yiyang Du, Yuxuan Kuang
  *Evaluates how well frontier multimodal agents (such as GPT-6 Astra) can skip high-level primitives and control robots directly with low-level numeric actions, noting that episode success rates alone conceal failure details.*
- **[TideCache: Efficient Hybrid LLM Serving with Transparent Cache Management and Layer-Wise Streaming](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26619)** | cs.DC | Jinbin Luo, Xiaozhe Yao
  *An inference serving system for open-weight models with hybrid architectures; transparent cache management and layer-wise streaming cope with the cost of heterogeneous KV caches across global-attention layers and bounded-state layers.*
- **[SoK: Assessing the Evolution of LLM Capability in Software Vulnerability Research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26601)** | cs.CR | Amine Lbath, Manan Suri
  *Systematizes how LLM agents' capabilities have evolved in real-world software vulnerability discovery, validation and patching, bringing claims scattered across papers and vendor reports under a single evaluation.*
- **[Represented but misread: Post-training changes how language models read out what they represent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26584)** | cs.CL | Jochen Laubrock
  *Compares linear probes and self-answer results across six open-weight base models and their post-trained versions, finding that post-training mostly changes the "readout" rather than the internal representations themselves.*
