*(The 5 most relevant to agents / reasoning / LLM training / inference)*

- **[AgentSSL: Can MLE Agents Leverage Unlabeled Data?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17323)** | cs.AI | Ethan Lin, Akanksha Sarkar
  *MLE agents had only been evaluated in fully supervised settings; this paper is the first systematic test of whether they can use unlabeled data, and identifies annotation and pipeline engineering as the two main barriers to deployment.*
- **[CoReMem: Cost-Internalizing Memory for Successive Coding Tasks with Prospective Valuation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17316)** | cs.AI | Peiwen Li, Harshit Verma
  *Coding agents must write memories ahead of time on successive tasks; the authors internalize "write/read cost vs future payoff" so the agent itself decides what to remember and when to act.*
- **[Classical Energies Learn to Reason 1,000x Faster than Looped Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17309)** | cs.AI | Benjamin Hoover, Hugh Blayney
  *On Sudoku- and maze-style reasoning, replacing looped-model training with energy descent is three orders of magnitude faster and more stable.*
- **[ArchSpace: Recording, Reviewing, and Referencing LLM Architecture Research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17332)** | cs.CL | Jie Wang, Tong Zhu
  *Architectural innovations in DeepSeek, Qwen and Kimi are rarely recorded in full; this paper offers a reproducible, citable framework for recording LLM architecture research.*
- **[Ballad: Bandit-Based LLM Routing for Automated Heuristic Discovery](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17241)** | cs.AI | Samidha Verma, Ankit Anand
