- **[MDSKILL: Learning to Internalize Skill Memory through Multi-View Distillation for Efficient Dynamic Agent Self-Evolution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05313)** | cs.CL | Dian Jin, Jinluan Yang
  *Uses multi-view distillation to internalize "skill memory" from text prompts into policy parameters, replacing pure-RL skill bootstrapping and speeding up the training of self-evolving agents.*

  *In frontier open-weight models, later tokens can cost up to 7× as much to generate as early ones; with frequent context compaction, long-horizon and long-input tasks lose almost no accuracy.*

  *A position paper: a decade spent measuring AI by how much machines can do on their own should give way to human–machine cognitive symbiosis rather than autonomy for its own sake.*
