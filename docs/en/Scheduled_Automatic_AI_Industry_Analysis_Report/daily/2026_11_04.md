## 📄 arXiv Papers — cs.AI / cs.LG / cs.CL

- **[WebHarbor: Docking Real Websites for Evolving GUI Agent Environments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03634)** | cs.AI | Zhaoyang Wang, Qianhui Wu
  *"Docks" real websites into reusable, evolvable GUI agent environments, sidestepping the old problems of drifting content on live sites, restricted access, hard-to-cover logged-in states, and synthetic environments that feel fake.*
- **[Strategist: Evolving Reasoning with Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03618)** | cs.AI | Taiming Lu, Qi Xu
  *Argues that "the model is only half of an agent system, the other half is the reasoning strategy": proposes a meta-agent to evolve strategies automatically instead of hand-writing one per project.*
- **[Not All Reasoning Generalizes: A Case Study on Learning Generalizable Reasoning Strategies in RLVR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03627)** | cs.LG | Ziqi Pang, Yu-Xiong Wang
  *Tests whether reasoning strategies learned under RLVR transfer across tasks using a controllable equation-solving framework; the answer is "not all reasoning generalizes".*
- **[FADE: Factored Ability–Difficulty Filtering for Online RLVR Scheduling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03625)** | cs.LG | Tomas Duncun, Jonah Ellison
  *An online RLVR scheduler: filters out prompt groups that are all-correct or all-wrong (zero advantage) and spends the generation budget on informative samples.*
- **[Sonar-MoE: In-Backward Expert Updates for Memory-Efficient Mixture-of-Experts Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03577)** | cs.LG | Avni Mittal, Avinash Anand
  *Updates experts one by one during backpropagation instead of stacking every expert's gradients in VRAM until the optimizer step, sharply cutting the memory pressure of MoE training as it scales.*
