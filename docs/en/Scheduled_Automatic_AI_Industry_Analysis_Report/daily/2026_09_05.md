  *CoT reasoning chains "looking readable ≠ being interpretable": comparing human-judged importance with what the model actually relies on, challenging the popular practice of treating reasoning chains as a window into transparency.*

  *A sequel in the on-policy distillation (OPD) series: student self-sampled rollouts + teacher token-level supervision, exploring the limits of distillation with a single training example, aimed at small open-source models catching up to large ones.*
- **[Clean Engineering, Unstable Measurement: A Preregistered Reliability Failure of Black-Box LLM Observers on Shared Endpoints](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04198)** | cs.AI | Haoyaun Zhu, Jie Zhang
  *A preregistered replication shows that black-box LLM judges on shared endpoints give unreliable measurements—yet they are already used to filter training data and rank leaderboards; the measuring instrument itself is unstable.*
- **[Knowledge Acquisition During Pre-training? Large Language Models Learn Better With Auxiliary Views](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04180)** | cs.CL | Joseph Lee, Yidi Huang
  *Research on knowledge acquisition during pre-training: feeding data as "auxiliary views" (content reformulations / multiple perspectives) helps LLMs learn more solidly, pointing to more efficient pre-training data construction.*
