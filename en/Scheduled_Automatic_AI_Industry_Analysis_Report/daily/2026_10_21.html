📄 arXiv Papers — cs.AI / cs.LG / cs.CL

  • Train An Imaginative Agent | cs.AI | Jiaxin Ge, Junyi Zhang, Tianning Chai Proposes Agentic Imagination (Agima), a training framework that makes agents "imagine" consequences before acting. The motivation is concrete — in Sokoban a single plausible-looking box push turns the endgame into a dead end, while real environment feedback is sparse and actions are irreversible.
  • Agent World Model in the Wild: Computer-Use Agent Environment Synthesis | cs.AI | Zhaoyang Wang, Qianhui Wu, Haorui Wang WildAWM: scalably synthesizes "controllable environments + tasks" to train general computer-use agents, sidestepping the safety risks of training at scale inside real systems.
  • WeAct: Dynamic Weight Switching as Agent Actions | cs.LG | Rui Shi, Xunjian Yin, Jiacheng Sang Long-horizon agents must learn heterogeneous abilities across interleaved trajectories, and shared parameter updates cause catastrophic forgetting. WeAct treats "switching weights" itself as an agent action, so different stages use different parameter configurations.
  • LatComm: Reusable Context Compression for Latent Communication between LLMs | cs.CL | Huawei Lin, Jinting Liu, Tan Yu When multiple models collaborate, the same context gets passed to different LLMs over and over and re-tokenized each time. LatComm learns a compact latent representation that is reusable across questions and legible to other models.
  • Text-to-Image Generative Modeling Without Language Pretraining | cs.LG | Keshigeyan Chandrasegaran, Yue Zhao, Armin W. Thomas Halcyon: a family of early-fusion multimodal autoregressive models pretrained entirely from scratch (100M image-text pairs, the GPIC dataset), testing the default assumption that strong T2I requires internet-scale language pretraining.
